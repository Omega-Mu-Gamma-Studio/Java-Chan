# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Java-chan is a student-built, open-source educational project. We want it
to be a welcoming place to contribute, ask questions, and learn — whether
you're fixing a typo in lesson 3.7 or proposing a structural change to the
phase engine.

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience level,
nationality, appearance, race, religion, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, PRs, and discussions.
- Assume good faith — most confusing PRs or blunt comments are haste, not
  malice.
- Give specific, actionable feedback on contributions rather than just
  "this is wrong."
- Respect the maintainers' time — this is a side project run by students,
  not a company with a support team.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in any project space
  (issues, PRs, discussions, or anywhere else representing the project).
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional or academic setting.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (this repo's issues, PRs,
discussions) and in public spaces when someone is representing the
project or the [Omega Mu Gamma Studio](https://github.com/Omega-Mu-Gamma-Studio)
org.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the
[Omega Mu Gamma Studio GitHub org](https://github.com/Omega-Mu-Gamma-Studio),
or directly to [@albertofelix08](https://github.com/albertofelix08).
Reports will be reviewed and addressed as promptly as a small
student-run project reasonably can — expect a real response, not
necessarily an instant one.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues/PRs, or block contributors
who violate this policy, at their discretion.
